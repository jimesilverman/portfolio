They cover businesses by industry vertical: ranging from globally-known celebrities and media productions to solar installations and marine contractors.

April 2018 – August 2021

Staying consistent during change.

I joined in 2018 as the third designer on the team, helping pioneer the UX practice and introduce user-centered design to a traditionally conservative industry.

Throughout these changes, I stayed grounded in core UX principles and processes, leading efforts such as:

  • Conducting continuous user research to uncover needs, identify opportunities, and validate solutions.
  • Creating rapid prototypes and production-ready mockups for swift implementation.
  • Designing and documenting a new design system while building multiple products in parallel.
  • Partnering with cross-functional teams to ensure solutions aligned with user needs, business goals, and technical feasibility.

Case Studies

At ProSight, I drove digital transformation that streamlined underwriting, claims, and quoting processes. My work on self-service and reporting tools empowered partner agencies to operate more efficiently—freeing internal teams to focus on complex, high-value cases.
