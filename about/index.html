Decades designing for enterprises.

I’ve spent my career in the gray areas of high-scale enterprise SaaS, Fintech, and Insurtech. From modernizing legacy CRMs for 18,000 users to architecting the integration of Agentic AI, my focus remains constant: turning chaotic, technical debt-heavy systems into tools that actually empower people to do their best work.

As a hands-on lead, I operate at the intersection of product, engineering, and user needs. I am equally comfortable defining high-level strategy with executives as I am architecting complex system flows and mentoring design teams to elevate their collective craft.

I focus on systems, strategy, and scale.

My work goes beyond designing individual features. I specialize in enterprise-scale modernization by establishing a clear vision and driving the cross-functional consensus required to ship it. I thrive when I can dive into ambiguous, messy requirements and extract a cohesive product strategy that balances technical feasibility with human-centered principles.

My goal is to act as a multiplier for the design practice. Whether I am architecting a new design system, mentoring fellow designers, or establishing better cross-functional processes, I bring a balance of strategic vision and hands-on craft to ensure teams deliver scalable, high-impact results.

How I like to get things done.

These principles shape how I collaborate, make decisions, and approach challenges. If you find yourself nodding along, we’ll get along just fine.

Lead by example.

Real leadership is founded upon trust, and actions speak louder than slide decks.

Align first, design second.

A beautiful interface is useless if engineering can't build it or the business doesn't need it.

Do less, not more.

I don't just want to solve the immediate problem. I look for patterns to build scalable foundations that accelerate future work.

Hold opinions loosely.

Changing your opinion is just a sign that you've learned something new.

Embrace the chaos.

Enterprise problems are rarely neat. I thrive on turning complex, ambiguous requirements into a clear, actionable product vision.

Have fun doing it.

Let's work together.

Like what you see? Great to hear! Even if I’m not actively on the job hunt, I’m always curious about interesting challenges and love hearing about opportunities to make an impact.

Here’s what I look for in a role:

  • Remote-first: I work best from my own environment. Having spent years in the 4-hour commute grind, a remote-first culture is non-negotiable for my productivity and my family.
  • Deep Complexity: I love turning chaotic, complicated interfaces into experiences that feel clear, simple, and efficient.
  • Workflow Products: I’m happiest when I’m making someone’s day easier, their work faster, and their tools more enjoyable to use.
  • Individual contributor: I want to lead the work, not the org chart. I thrive when I can shape the big-picture vision and mentor the team without the overhead of people management.
